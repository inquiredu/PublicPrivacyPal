## Result

The capture form preserves the conservative all-documents default and offers exact adopted measure
and clause choices, including visible disabled/obsolete selections. A coverage preview explains
which declared sources remain unresolved without creating a finding or score. Required and excluded
documents retain their pasted text, dates and declarations. Exclusions require an explicit reason
and action; the action says that it clears the selected dependencies. Returning to required coverage
retains the earlier reason until the reviewer explicitly clears it.

Linked material can identify another manifest document, including an unavailable addendum, or retain
an explained exclusion. Changing an excluded link to included does not silently discard its reason.
Removing a link declaration leaves its document intact. Empty documents with referenced links or
edited dependencies cannot be removed through the empty-document shortcut.

Changing review scope preserves every document's earlier scope and applicability. An explicit
“Use review scope and reassess applicability” action copies the scope and resets applicability to
unresolved. Required documents with mismatched scope block client submission. An explained excluded
document may retain its separate scope. Saved source views use the adopted human-readable measure,
clause and document labels, and distinguish included unavailable sources from excluded links.
